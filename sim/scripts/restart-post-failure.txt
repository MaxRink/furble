# Negative fixture for the restart seam (plan 156). A failure raised after the
# `restart` step must be the run's status: the rebooted process runs the rest
# of the script, and its failing assert exits 1 rather than being masked by the
# re-exec chain. Kept here rather than under sim/scenarios because every
# manifest suite declares a fixed status (0, or the parser's 2 for the invalid
# fixtures) and this one exits 1. run-invalid.sh runs it.
wait 200
assert ui.page main
restart
wait 300
assert ui.page settings
exit
