# GPS is off by default, and the disabled task must not poll.
#
# The task used to delay 100 ms and loop, ten scheduler wakes per second for no
# work. It now blocks on its queue until enable() or disable() wakes it. The
# simulator counts entries to the disabled branch.

wait 300
assert gps.enabled 0
wait 5000
assert_max gps.idle_wakes 5
exit
