# StickS3: the Port A rail is not the external 5 V net.
#
# The StickS3 5 V boost feeds Grove, EXT_5V and the IR pair from one PM1 output,
# but that is the PM1 boost rather than a separate external net furble can drop,
# so the model reports the collateral question as not applicable rather than
# inventing a BUS_5V state. The GPS rail itself still switches.
seed hw_profile sticks3
wait 300

assert board.gps_rail yes
assert power.rail.gps off
assert power.rail.ext5v n/a
assert power.rail.ext5v_drops n/a

action gps-rail-on
assert power.rail.gps on
assert power.rail.ext5v n/a

action gps-rail-off
assert power.rail.gps off
assert power.rail.ext5v n/a
assert power.rail.ext5v_drops n/a
exit
