# A MIA-M10Q runs its UART at 38400 (MIA-M10Q data sheet UBX-22015849 Table
# 21). The Auto ladder finds the rate from its NMEA, and only then does the
# MON-VER poll go out, so the receiver is identified at the locked rate.
seed gps true
seed gps_baud auto
seed gps_receiver 38400
seed gps_model m10-mia
seed gps_m10_hot true
wait 12000
assert gps.receiver present
assert gps.baud 38400
assert gps.backend ubx
assert gps.ubx.module MIA-M10Q
assert-eventually 3000 gps.config.1.state ACKED
assert gps.ubx.verified 1
assert-eventually 3000 gps.from_pvt 1
exit
