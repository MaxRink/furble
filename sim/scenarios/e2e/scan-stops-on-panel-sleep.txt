# Power lane P-002: a sleeping panel does not leave the scan receiving.
#
# With the display-off timer at its shortest setting the panel sleeps while the
# Scan page is open. The scan must stop at that point rather than keep the
# receiver at full duty for the whole panel-off period.

seed inactivity 1
seed display_off 1
wait 300

action nav scan
wait 500
assert ui.page scan
assert scan.active yes

# The inactivity timer sleeps the panel at 30 s and that must stop the scan.
wait 31000
assert scan.active no
exit
