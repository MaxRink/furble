# The Unicore gaps, reported rather than faked. UC R1.5 documents no standby
# command and no dynamic platform model, so a standby policy or a platform
# setting leaves the receiver alone. A flashless product refuses CFGSAVE, and a
# part that answers nothing never identifies at all and keeps the NMEA backend.
# Plan 388.
seed gps true
seed gps_model unicore
seed gps_power 1
seed gps_duty 10
seed gps_platform 3
seed gps_gnss 1
seed gps_rate 1
wait 8000
assert gps.backend unicore

# The configuration went out and was acknowledged; the standby and the platform
# were refused before either reached the UART.
assert-eventually 5000 gps.config.0.state ACKED
assert text.fails 0
assert text.resets 1
assert text.saved 0
exit
