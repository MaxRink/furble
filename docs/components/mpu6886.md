# TDK InvenSense MPU-6886

**Part and package.** TDK InvenSense MPU-6886, a six-axis MotionTracking device
with a three-axis gyroscope and a three-axis accelerometer. Package and pinout
were not read and are UNVERIFIED.

**Role in furble.** The IMU and wake-on-motion source on the StickC family. The
board support library owns the normal IMU path, and furble arms wake on motion
directly so a motion event can wake the board
(`src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:15` to `:66`).

**Documents.** [MPU6886-DS]

## Facts still unverified

- The revision. The document is DS-000193 revision 1.1 and revision 1.2 does not
  exist, so the comment at `src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:17` to `:18` is wrong.
  That is a code defect recorded here, not a documentation gap.
- The mg per LSB of the wake-on-motion threshold. Sections 8.20 to 8.22 give no
  scale, so the 4 mg claim is UNVERIFIED. At the plus or minus 8 g range the
  driver selects with `ACCEL_CONFIG` 0x10 the effective threshold may be about
  four times the intended value, which would make motion wake less sensitive
  than the comment claims.
- The 64 mg equivalence, which follows from the unverified 4 mg scale.
- The exact bit positions claimed for WOM_X, WOM_Y and WOM_Z in INT_STATUS.
- The latch bit position in INT_PIN_CFG, and whether it is bit 5.
- The accelerometer low power current and the sleep current.
- Any errata.
