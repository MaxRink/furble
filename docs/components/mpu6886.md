# TDK InvenSense MPU-6886

**Part and package.** TDK InvenSense MPU-6886, a six-axis MotionTracking device
with a three-axis gyroscope and a three-axis accelerometer in a 3 mm by 3 mm by
0.75 mm 24-pin LGA. Package drawing and pinout were not read and are UNVERIFIED.

**Status.** shipped. `m5stick-c`, `m5stick-c-plus`, `m5stick-c-plus2`,
`m5stack-core2` and a M5Tough running the Core2 image carry the part, and
`src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp` arms wake-on-motion on all of them.

**Role in furble.** The IMU and wake-on-motion source on the StickC family. The
board support library owns the normal IMU path, and furble arms wake on motion
directly so a motion event can wake the board
(`src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:18` to `:75`).

**Documents.** [MPU6886-DS]

Row 6 is the one that stays open, and now for a stated reason rather than a
missing page. The driver comment at `src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:48` to `:54`
says one threshold count is 4 mg and that 0x10 is about 64 mg, and since #504 it
marks the figure as an assumption rather than a datasheet value. The scale itself
lives in `include/FurbleIMUMotionScale.h` as
`MPU6886_WOM_MG_PER_COUNT_ASSUMED`. The document does
not say that. Sections 8.20 to 8.22 are three lines long each and give no scale,
the register map gives no scale, and no other page in the 59 mentions mg per LSB
or a threshold resolution. So the 4 mg figure and the 64 mg equivalence are the
driver's own arithmetic or a figure from the vendor example code, and neither is
a datasheet value.

Row 7 makes that worse rather than better. The driver writes 0x10 to ACCEL_CONFIG
at `src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:121`, which sets ACCEL_FS_SEL to 10b, the plus
or minus 8 g range. If the
4 mg scale belongs to the plus or minus 2 g range, then at 8 g the effective
threshold is about four times the intended value. Motion wake would then be less
sensitive than the comment claims. Nothing in the document settles which range
the scale belongs to, so row 6 is UNVERIFIED and this consequence is recorded
rather than resolved.

### Two vendor defects in section 5.1

Both are in the arm sequence on page 24, and the driver is right where the
document is wrong.

| Defect | What page 24 says | What the rest of the document says |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Step 4 names the wrong register | "Set the motion threshold in ACCEL_WOM_THR register (0x1F)" | Table 15 has no 0x1F entry at all. The register map jumps from 0x1D ACCEL_CONFIG 2 to 0x23 FIFO_EN. The three threshold registers are ACCEL_WOM_X_THR, ACCEL_WOM_Y_THR and ACCEL_WOM_Z_THR at 0x20, 0x21 and 0x22, and sections 8.20 to 8.22 confirm it |
| The step list skips a number | Step 5 is followed by Step 7. There is no Step 6 | No step is missing from the procedure in substance: step 7 sets the wake-up frequency through SMPLRT_DIV. The numbering is simply wrong |

The driver follows Table 15 and sections 8.20 to 8.22, so `WOM_X_THRESHOLD` 0x20,
`WOM_Y_THRESHOLD` 0x21 and `WOM_Z_THRESHOLD` 0x22 are right and page 24's step 4
is a vendor error. A driver written from the step list alone would write 0x1F,
which on this part is not a threshold register.

## Why the disarm path restores registers

`disarm()` puts `ACCEL_CONFIG_2`, `SAMPLE_RATE_DIVIDER` and `INT_PIN_CONFIG`
back to M5Unified's own values, taken from the init table in `MPU6886_Class.cpp`.
Without that, the spirit level and the IMU live page keep running at the
wake-on-motion sample rate and filter after the engine is gone
(`src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:47-57`).

## The clear-on-read trap

The interrupt pin is the only motion signal furble owns. `INT_STATUS` at 0x3A is
clear-on-read and M5Unified reads it on every IMU update (`MPU6886_Class.cpp:253`),
so the spirit level, the IMU live page and the console probe would all consume
WOM events if the register were the source of truth. That is why the latched pin
(`INT_PIN_CONFIG` bit 5) is used instead (`src/FurbleIMUMotionMPU6886.cpp:68-75`).
