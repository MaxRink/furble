# Texas Instruments TCA8418

**Part and package.** TI TCA8418, a keypad scan IC with up to 10 columns by 8
rows, 80 keys, with ESD protection. The pin list on page 3 is ROW0 to ROW7,
COL0 to COL9, INT, SCL, SDA, RESET, VCC and GND in a 24-pin WQFN. There is no
address pin. Package pitch was not read and is UNVERIFIED.

**Role in furble.** Planned, not driven. The Cardputer Adv and the T-LoRa Pager
have keyboards, and the pinned board support library has no TCA8418 support, so
the plan expects a new driver (`plans/322-hardware-abstraction.md`, phase 1
audit, TCA8418 row).

**Status.** future. No code drives it; the Cardputer Adv and the Pager are the boards that would.

Rows 11 and 13 record two absences, which is why they are worded as they are.
A reader who expects a scan-rate register from another keypad part, or an INT
polarity bit, will not find one. The interrupt is active low with no way to
invert it, and the scan rate is not programmable.

Row 10 matters more than it looks. The only debounce control the part offers is
to turn the two-stage filter off per pin. Every other part in this corpus that
debounces a button lets the host set the time, and this one does not.
