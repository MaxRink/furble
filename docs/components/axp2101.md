# X-Powers AXP2101

**Part and package.** X-Powers AXP2101, a single-cell NVDC PMU with an E-gauge
fuel gauge, in a QFN package with an exposed pad. Package drawing is section 8
of the datasheet ([AXP2101-DS], section 8.1 Package), pad count and pitch were
not read for this page and are UNVERIFIED.

**Role in furble.** The register driver for boards the pinned board support
library does not know. M5 boards keep the library's own AXP2101 path. The
driver covers the subset of rails, ADC readings, the fuel gauge, the backup
cell charger and the power key that a non-M5 board needs
(`plans/322-hardware-abstraction.md`, phase 1 audit).

**Documents.** [AXP2101-DS]

### Vendor defect in section 6.13.2.75

The vendor revision 1.0 table at section 6.13.2.75, page 53, labels REG 90 bit 4
"aldo1" a second time, duplicating bit 0. The driver reads bit 4 as BLDO1,
because BLDO1 is the next LDO in the enable map and the six voltage registers
92 to 97 follow the ALDO1-4, BLDO1, BLDO2 order. The comment at
`lib/hal/AXP2101.cpp:39` to `:41` records that reading, and it is load bearing:
delete it and the next reader will "fix" bit 4 back to ALDO1 and switch the
wrong rail. The defect is REPORTED from the independent pass. This corpus did
not reopen the table, and the row for `REG_LDO_EN0` stays CITED.

## Facts still unverified

- The per-register subsection numbers 6.13.2.1 to 6.13.2.88. Section 6.13.2
  exists and is the register description, but the retrieved text stopped before
  the register bodies, so no individual subsection number could be read. Every
  row that cites one is CITED, not VERIFIED.
- The 0x4A identity value and register 0x03. The revision 1.0 map defines no
  identity register at all, so the pair is UNVERIFIED by the vendor document
  and its source is XPowersLib, named in the citation gap row.
- The exact wording of section 6.5.4 about RTCLDO staying on in the off state.
  Only the heading "6.5.4 System power on/off management" was read.
- The claim that code 31 is reserved in the LDO voltage encoding.
- The gauge enable bit position (REG 18 bit 3) and the stale-value behaviour the
  driver describes for a disabled gauge.
- Package pad count, pitch and exposed pad size.
- An errata document. None was found, and the driver carries no erratum.
