## Review: request changes

Reviewed at head `647bb806`, base `origin/master` = `baa4ac23`, in a worktree under `/tmp`. Nothing pushed to this branch. A per-finding write-up is also in `plans/549-review-feedback.md` locally in that worktree (not pushed, per the review rules).

The premise is right and most of the hardware research is right: every pin I could check against a vendor document matches, and both corrections to the brief (no TCA8418; the Ultra does have onboard GNSS) are correct. What does not hold is the claim that the M5 side is untouched, and the claim that the LilyGO arm only lacks a build for the two new environments. The seam removed the only definition of `Display::screen()` that M5 builds had, one LilyGO arm has never been compiled by anything, and the flagship input path drops most keyboard presses by construction.

### Verified, no change needed

* Every T-Deck pin: GPIO10 gate, I2C 18/8, KB INT 46, I2S 7/5/6, SD CS 39, panel CS 12 / DC 11 / BL 42 / no reset, SPI 41/38/40, trackball 2/3/1/15/0, battery ADC 4. Source: `LilyGoLib/variants/lilygo_tdeck/pins_arduino.h` and `T-Deck/examples/UnitTest/utilities.h`.
* T-Deck panel: native 240x320, landscape at rotation 1, inverted. Source: `LilyGoLib/src/display/TDeckDisplayConfig.h` (`0x21` INVON and `0x60` MV|MX in the ST7789 init and rotation table).
* Keyboard: 0x55 slave, 100 kHz, one byte per request, Enter 0x0D, Backspace 0x08, Alt+C 0x0C. Source: `T-Deck/examples/Keyboard_ESP32C3/Keyboard_ESP32C3.ino` (`I2C_DEV_ADDR 0x55`, `Wire.begin(..., 100000UL)`, the three byte assignments).
* No TCA8418 and no arrow keys: correct, and `lib/hal` holds only AXP2101, BQ27220, SX1262, XL9555.
* Every Ultra pin: QSPI SCLK 40 / D0 38 / D1 39 / D2 42 / D3 45 / CS 41 / RST 37, I2C 3/2, I2S 9/10/11, SD CS 21, shared SPI 35/33/34, GNSS 43/44/PPS 13, BOOT GPIO0, expander enable 7. Source: `LilyGoLib/variants/lilygo_twatch_ultra/pins_arduino.h`.
* Ultra addresses, rails and cell: AXP2101 0x34, XL9555 0x20, ALDO1 SD, ALDO2 display, BLDO1 GNSS, BLDO2 speaker, VRTC GNSS backup, 1100 mAh. Source: `LilyGoLib/docs/hardware/lilygo-t-watch-ultra.md`.
* `Board::compiledProfile()` keeps the M5 side in an adapter: the LilyGO arms name no M5 symbol, and `M5.getBoard()` stays in `src/FurbleBoard.cpp`, a declared adapter. `check_coupling_ratchet.py --base origin/master`: clean.
* `sdCsPin` is consistent on every row: all nine rows specify 27 initialisers, the new rows carry 39 and 21, every M5 row carries -1, and the targets that compile the table build with `-Wall -Wextra -Werror` (`tests/host/CMakeLists.txt:3092`, `:3106`), so a short initialiser would fail rather than quietly default the pin to 0.

### Verdict

Not mergeable as it stands. Findings 1 and 2 are compile and link breaks that no target in this PR exercises, and 1 is in the M5 path the PR says it did not touch. Finding 3 makes the T-Deck keyboard, the reason to port this board at all, mostly unresponsive. Before merge: one M5 env must link, `src/FurbleSD.cpp` must compile under both LilyGO macros in a target that includes it, both new envs must build with recorded size and stack numbers and matching budget entries, the keyboard latch must survive a 33 ms read cadence with a test that fails today, and the Ultra's visible area and orientation must be recorded from a board rather than from the facts row the simulator mirrors.
